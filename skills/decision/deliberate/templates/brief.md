# Structured Brief — Phase 1 Output Format

Use this structure for Phase 1 independent analysis. Each expert produces one brief.

```markdown
## Top 3 Constraints
1. [Constraint 1 — the most limiting factor the data reveals]
2. [Constraint 2]
3. [Constraint 3]

## Top 3 Priorities
1. [Priority 1 — what matters most for the question, ranked]
2. [Priority 2]
3. [Priority 3]

## Biggest Risk
[The danger others might miss — be specific, cite the data]

## Confidence
[HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW] — [one sentence: why this confidence level]
```

Rules:
- Be specific. Cite actual values from the data (e.g., "<marker> <value>, <trend>%" not "low <marker>").
- Constraints are facts about the person's situation. Priorities are what to do about them.
- The "Biggest Risk" should be something another expert might overlook.
- ≤300 words total.
