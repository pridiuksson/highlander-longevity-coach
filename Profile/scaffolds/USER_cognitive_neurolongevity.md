### Cognitive Neurolongevity & Brain Health Architecture

- **Vitality Ceiling (Neuroplasticity, Glymphatic Clearance & Executive Stamina):**
  - Neurotrophic induction & Hippocampal neurogenesis (Cotman & Berchtold, *Trends Neurosci* 2002): High-intensity aerobic intervals, endurance exercise, and complex motor skill acquisition stimulate circulating brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), enhancing synaptic plasticity, dendritic spine density, and spatial memory.
  - Glymphatic metabolic waste clearance (Xie, Nedergaard et al., *Science* 2013): Slow-wave deep sleep (N3 delta stage) facilitates convective fluid exchange between cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) and interstitial fluid (ISF), clearing neurotoxic waste products including amyloid-beta and hyperphosphorylated tau; optimize sleep environment to protect slow-wave sleep duration.
  - Autonomic nervous system resilience & Vagal tone (Thayer et al., *Neurosci Biobehav Rev* 2009): Track vagal nerve modulation via nocturnal heart rate variability; higher parasympathetic vagal tone directly reflects superior prefrontal cortex executive control, emotional regulation, and physiological stress-buffering capacity.
  - Cognitive stamina & Neurotransmitter dynamics: Calibrate daily deep-work focus blocks to protect dopamine receptor sensitivity; implement circadian daylight exposure within thirty minutes of waking to optimize cortisol awakening response and elevate daytime vigilance.
- **Clinical Floor (Neurovascular Defense & Systemic Guardrails):**
  - Neurovascular inflammation & Methylation kinetics:
    - Target high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP) under 0.5 mg/L to prevent chronic low-grade systemic neuroinflammation.
    - Fasting plasma homocysteine: Target concentration under nine to ten micromoles per liter to prevent microvascular cerebral endothelial damage and hippocampal atrophy; ensure adequate vitamin B12, folate, and B6 intake (Smith & Refsum, *Am J Clin Nutr* 2016).
  - Nocturnal cerebral oxygenation: Screen for obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) using the STOP-BANG clinical assessment; eliminate nocturnal hypoxic episodes which accelerate cognitive decline.
