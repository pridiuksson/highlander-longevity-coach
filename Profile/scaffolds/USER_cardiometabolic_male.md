### Male Midlife Cardiometabolic & Endocrine Architecture

- **Vitality Ceiling (Salutogenesis, Bioenergetics & Cardiorespiratory Reserve):**
  - Cardiorespiratory fitness longevity tiering (Mandsager et al., *JAMA Netw Open* 2018): Cardiorespiratory fitness demonstrates an inverse, graded association with all-cause mortality with no observed upper limit of benefit; the elite fitness tier is defined at or above the 97.7th percentile of age-matched cohorts.
  - Mitochondrial fatty acid oxidation & Aerobic base: 150 to 200 minutes weekly in blood lactate range of 1.5 to 2.0 mmol/L (San Millán & Brooks, *Sports Med* 2018) maximizing metabolic flexibility, mitochondrial reticular density, and visceral fat clearance.
  - Visceral fat eradication & Glycemic resilience: Target waist-to-height ratio under 0.50, visceral adipose tissue (VAT) area under 100 square centimeters on DEXA scan, and HOMA-IR under 1.2 (fasting insulin and fasting glucose).
- **Clinical Floor (Harm Reduction, Atherogenic Burden & Androgen Safety):**
  - Atherogenic particles & Cumulative lifetime exposure:
    - ApoB target: `<APOB_TARGET>` mg/dL (ESC/EAS guidelines: under 80 mg/dL for high risk, under 65 mg/dL for very high risk; corresponding LDL-C targets of under 70 and under 55 mg/dL).
    - Causal foundation: Mendelian randomization (Ference et al., 2017, 2018) demonstrates that lifetime cumulative exposure to ApoB-containing lipoproteins drives atherogenesis; clinical hazard reduction is proportional to absolute lifetime reduction in particle burden.
    - Lipoprotein(a) [Lp(a)]: `<LPA_VALUE>` nmol/L (NLA and ESC/EAS consensus: screen at least once per lifetime; values exceeding 125 nmol/L indicate independently elevated lifetime ASCVD hazard).
  - Subclinical coronary plaque burden (Imaging & Triage):
    - Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) Agatston score: `<CAC_SCORE>` on `<DATE>`.
      - *Clinical triage:* CAC of 0 indicates low 10-year risk in absence of smoking or high Lp(a); CAC of 1 to 99 warrants lifestyle optimization and consideration of preventive pharmacotherapy; CAC of 100 or greater indicates significant plaque burden and triggers intensive lipid-lowering therapy per ACC/AHA guidelines.
    - Computed Tomography Coronary Angiography (CTCA): Soft plaque and stenosis status: `<ASSESSED / NOT ASSESSED>` (SCCT guidelines: evaluates non-calcified, vulnerable lipid-rich plaque before calcification develops).
    - Resting hemodynamics: Seated, rested blood pressure target with systolic under 120 and diastolic under 80 mmHg.
  - Hepatic steatosis & Advanced fibrosis triage:
    - Hepatic steatosis (MASLD) is assessed via ultrasound, FibroScan CAP score, or MRI-PDFF.
    - FIB-4 index: Calculated from age, AST, ALT, and platelets as a triage tool specifically to rule out advanced hepatic fibrosis (score under 1.30 provides greater than 90% negative predictive value for advanced F3–F4 fibrosis).
  - Male endocrine & Androgen profile (Bhasin et al., 2018):
    - Fasting morning serum markers: Total and free fractions, with Sex Hormone-Binding Globulin (SHBG) and albumin to calculate bioavailable androgen availability per Endocrine Society clinical practice guidelines.
    - Diagnostic prerequisites & Safety screening:
      - Confirm low levels on at least two separate morning fasting blood draws (between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM) due to circadian and acute illness fluctuations.
      - Rule out reversible causes (obstructive sleep apnea, severe caloric restriction, visceral obesity, chronic sleep deprivation).
      - Baseline hematocrit: Hematocrit exceeding 50% warrants caution; hematocrit exceeding 54% is an absolute contraindication to testosterone therapy due to erythrocytosis and hyperviscosity risk.
      - Baseline PSA and prostate examination: Screen for occult prostate malignancy prior to therapy.
      - Fertility intent: Exogenous androgen therapy suppresses gonadotropins and spermatogenesis; alternative therapies (hCG, SERMs) must be prioritized if future fertility is desired.
