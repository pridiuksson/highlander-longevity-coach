### Perimenopause & Post-Menopause Longevity Architecture

- **Vitality Ceiling (Salutogenesis, Osteogenic Loading & Neuroendocrine Vitality):**
  - High-Intensity Resistance & Impact Training (LIFTMOR Protocol; Watson et al., *J Bone Miner Res* 2018): Supervised twice-weekly HiRIT (deadlift, overhead barbell press, back squat at 80% to 85% 1RM, paired with jumping chin-ups with drop landings) stimulates bone mineral accretion, strengthens trabecular microarchitecture, and reverses age-related osteopenia.
  - Visceral adiposity & Mitochondrial metabolic flexibility: Zone 2 aerobic base volume (150 to 180 minutes weekly) paired with progressive mechanical loading preserves insulin sensitivity, counteracts post-estrogen visceral adiposity accumulation, and maintains cellular mitochondrial oxidative capacity.
  - Neuroendocrine & Vasomotor vitality: Targeted neuromodulatory strategies (including neurokinin 3 receptor antagonists such as Fezolinetant, temperature autoregulation, and sleep hygiene) preserve sleep architecture, emotional stability, and cognitive clarity during endocrine transitions.
- **Clinical Floor (Harm Reduction, Endocrine Safety & Bone Density Surveillance):**
  - Menopausal transition staging (STRAW+10 Framework; Harlow et al., *J Clin Endocrinol Metab* 2012):
    - Current stage: <REPRODUCTIVE (STAGE -3) / EARLY TRANSITION (STAGE -2) / LATE TRANSITION (STAGE -1) / EARLY POSTMENOPAUSE (STAGE +1) / LATE POSTMENOPAUSE (STAGE +2)>.
    - Diagnostic principle: Perimenopause is primarily a *clinical diagnosis* established by age (typically $> 40-45$) and menstrual cycle history. Stage -2 is marked by persistent cycle variability ($\ge 7$-day difference between consecutive cycles); Stage -1 is defined by an interval of amenorrhea $\ge 60$ days. Serum FSH measurements fluctuate erratically and are supportive rather than strictly required for diagnosis.
  - Menopausal Hormone Therapy (MHT / HRT) evaluation & Safety screen (NAMS 2022 Position Statement):
    - Regimen: <NONE / TRANSDERMAL 17-BETA ESTRADIOL + ORAL MICRONIZED PROGESTERONE / OTHER>.
    - Clinical indications: Indicated for bothersome vasomotor symptoms (VMS) and prevention of bone loss. Not indicated for primary or secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease or dementia.
    - Endometrial protection rule: Any woman with an intact uterus receiving systemic estrogen **must** receive adequate progestogen (e.g. oral micronized progesterone) to prevent endometrial hyperplasia and carcinoma.
    - Absolute contraindications screen (Must rule out prior to therapy):
      - Personal history of breast cancer or other estrogen-dependent malignancies.
      - Unexplained or undiagnosed abnormal uterine or vaginal bleeding.
      - Active or history of venous thromboembolism (DVT/PE) or known thrombophilic disorder.
      - Active or recent arterial thromboembolic disease (stroke, myocardial infarction, transient ischemic attack).
      - Active liver disease or severe hepatic impairment.
    - Route & Timing Hypothesis: Transdermal estradiol is preferred over oral estrogens due to absence of first-pass hepatic metabolism and negligible impact on pro-thrombotic clotting factors. Favorable benefit-risk profile is centered in women initiating within ten years of menopause onset or younger than age 60.
  - Bone Densitometry (ISCD Standards) & Fracture risk triage:
    - *Premenopausal and early transition (Stages -3 to -2):* Report **Z-scores** (normed to age/sex matched controls); Z-score below minus 2.0 indicates bone density below the expected range for age (ISCD Official Positions).
    - *Post-menopause (Stages +1 to +2):* Report **T-scores**; Lumbar spine: `<T_SCORE_LUMBAR>`, Femoral neck: `<T_SCORE_FEMUR>`, Total hip: `<T_SCORE_HIP>`.
    - LIFTMOR pre-screening contraindications: Pre-screen to rule out pre-existing vertebral fragility fractures, severe osteoporosis with high fracture risk, and symptomatic pelvic organ prolapse. Demands qualified in-person coaching supervision and progressive movement habituation before maximal loading.
  - Post-estrogen cardiometabolic & Lipid shifts:
    - Loss of endogenous estradiol attenuates hepatic LDL receptor clearance and promotes visceral fat accumulation. Monitor fasting ApoB, LDL particle number (LDL-P), triglycerides, and high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP).
    - Visceral adipose tissue (VAT): Target waist-to-height ratio under 0.50.
