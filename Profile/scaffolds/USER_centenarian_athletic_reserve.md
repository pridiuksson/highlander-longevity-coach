### Centenarian Athletic Reserve & Functional Mastery

- **Vitality Ceiling (The Centenarian Decathlon & Physiological Surplus):**
  - Multi-decade capacity surplus framework (Attia, *Medicine 3.0*; Harridge & Lazarus, *J Physiol* 2017): Physical functional capacity declines at approximately eight to ten percent per decade after age thirty, accelerating after age seventy. Building a twofold to threefold physiological surplus during midlife is the non-negotiable prerequisite to guarantee that at age 85, you maintain the functional capacity to lift luggage into an overhead bin, rise from the floor without assistance, carry groceries up stairs, and hike rough terrain.
  - The Centenarian Decathlon physical benchmarks:
    - *Aerobic endurance ceiling (Mandsager et al., 2018):* Establish peak cardiorespiratory fitness in the elite tier (at or above the 97.7th percentile of age-matched cohorts) so that multi-decade physiological regression never crosses below the functional frailty threshold.
    - *Loaded functional carries:* Farmer's walk carries with fifty to one hundred percent of body mass held across hands for continuous two-minute intervals.
    - *Grip and hanging endurance:* Passive and active bar hangs for ninety to one hundred twenty seconds in men, sixty to ninety seconds in women (powerful prognostic biomarker for all-cause functional longevity).
    - *Neuromuscular rate of force development (RFD):* Preserve explosive Type II motor unit firing frequency via dynamic medicine ball throws, kettlebell swings, and low-impact plyometrics (Reid & Fielding, *Exp Gerontol* 2012).
- **Clinical Floor (Orthopedic Preservation & Multi-Planar Integrity):**
  - Joint and connective tissue protection: Prioritize neutral spinal biomechanics and joint longevity over short-term maximal lifts; joint health is the true rate-limiting factor of athletic longevity.
  - Multi-planar dynamic stability: Train lateral and rotational movement planes (lateral lunges, Pallof presses, single-leg rotational reach) to maintain hip abductor stability and dynamic balance recovery reflexes.
