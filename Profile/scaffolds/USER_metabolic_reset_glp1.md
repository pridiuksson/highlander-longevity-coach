### Metabolic Reset & GLP-1 Lean Mass Preservation

- **Vitality Ceiling (Salutogenesis, Muscle Mass Defense & Metabolic Flexibility):**
  - Skeletal muscle mass defense & Hypertrophy maintenance (Prado et al., *Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol* 2024):
    - Clinical context (STEP-1 & SURMOUNT-1 trials): Up to 25% to 40% of total mass lost during GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist pharmacotherapy can represent lean tissue without structured intervention.
    - Sarcopenia prevention protocol: Progressive mechanical tension resistance training at least three days weekly targeting major compound movement patterns to protect metabolic rate, muscle quality, and functional capacity.
  - Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) optimization (Battelino et al., *Diabetes Care* 2019):
    - Time In Range (TIR): Target greater than 85% in optimal metabolic range (70 to 140 mg/dL for non-diabetic metabolic optimization; 70 to 180 mg/dL for diabetes management).
    - Glycemic variability (%CV): Target Coefficient of Variation at or below 36%, reflecting stable glucose excursions and low risk of reactive hypoglycemia.
    - Postprandial excursion boundary: Meal-related excursions kept under 30 mg/dL above pre-meal baseline.
  - Spontaneous physical activity floor (NEAT maintenance):
    - Non-negotiable daily movement floor of 8,000 to 10,000 steps to counteract central nervous system-mediated reductions in spontaneous energy expenditure during rapid weight loss.
- **Clinical Floor (Harm Reduction, Pharmacology Safety & Renal Screening):**
  - Therapeutic protocol & Pharmacology:
    - Compound & Dose: <DIET-ONLY / SEMAGLUTIDE / TIRZEPATIDE / RETATRUTIDE (INVESTIGATIONAL)>, `<CURRENT_DOSE_MG>`.
    - Regulatory status note: Semaglutide (GLP-1 RA) and Tirzepatide (GIP/GLP-1 RA) are approved for chronic weight management; Retatrutide is an investigational triple hormone receptor agonist undergoing Phase 3 clinical evaluation and is not currently approved for clinical use.
    - Absolute contraindications & Black Box safety screen:
      - Personal or family history of Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma (MTC) or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2).
      - Prior history of acute or chronic pancreatitis.
      - Severe gastrointestinal motility disorders or documented gastroparesis.
      - Pregnancy, lactation, or planned conception within two months (mandates a two-month preconception washout due to potential embryofetal toxicity).
  - Dietary protein floor & Renal baseline:
    - Target: 1.5 to 2.0 grams of protein per kilogram of target body weight daily, prioritized as the first component of each feeding.
    - Mandatory renal function baseline: Confirm baseline estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) prior to escalating protein intake to rule out underlying diabetic nephropathy.
  - Gastrointestinal motility, Fiber titration & Hydration safeguards:
    - Gastric emptying management: Avoid large boluses of coarse insoluble fiber, which can form obstructive phytobezoars in the presence of drug-induced gastroparesis. Gradually titrate dietary fiber up to 30 to 35 grams daily, prioritizing gentle soluble fibers.
    - Fluid and electrolyte floor: Fluid intake of 2.5 to 3.5 liters daily with sodium, potassium, and magnesium supplementation to offset suppressed thirst mechanisms and prevent dehydration-induced acute kidney injury (AKI).
    - Biliary vigilance: Monitor for right upper quadrant pain, as rapid weight loss independently increases the incidence of cholelithiasis and acute cholecystitis.
