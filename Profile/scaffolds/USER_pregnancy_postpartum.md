### Pregnancy & Postpartum Athletic Longevity

- **Vitality Ceiling (Salutogenesis, Functional Core Restoration & Impact Readiness):**
  - Deep core & Pelvic floor functional rehabilitation (Dufour et al., *J Women's Health Phys Ther* 2019):
    - Diastasis Recti Abdominis (DRA): Evaluate functional fascial tension-generating capacity and integrity of the linea alba under dynamic abdominal loading rather than static inter-recti distance alone.
    - Neuromuscular coordination: Progressive activation and full eccentric excursion of the levator ani and transversus abdominis prior to resuming high spinal axial compression.
  - Postnatal return-to-impact criteria (Goom, Donnelly & Brockwell, 2019):
    - Minimum timeline: Allow at least twelve weeks of postpartum tissue recovery before introducing running or high-impact ballistic activities.
    - Objective clinical milestones: Specialized pelvic health physiotherapy evaluation; absence of subjective urinary incontinence, pelvic organ prolapse symptoms (heaviness, dragging), or lumbopelvic pain; pain-free 30-minute continuous walking.
    - Impact-readiness strength battery: Ability to complete 20 single-leg calf raises, 20 single-leg bridges, 20 single-leg sit-to-stands, and 30 seconds of single-leg balance on each side without compensatory movement patterns.
  - Lactational metabolic surplus: Add 400 to 500 kilocalories daily during exclusive lactation to sustain milk synthesis without inducing maternal hypothalamic energy deficits.
- **Clinical Floor (Harm Reduction, Perinatal Safety & Red Flag Surveillance):**
  - Perinatal timeline & Obstetric clearance (ACOG 804 & Mottola et al., 2018):
    - Current status: <FIRST TRIMESTER / SECOND TRIMESTER / THIRD TRIMESTER / EARLY POSTPARTUM (WEEKS 0–12) / EXTENDED POSTPARTUM>.
    - Medical clearance: Uncomplicated pregnancy confirmed by obstetrician/midwife; absence of absolute contraindications (e.g. hemodynamically significant heart disease, incompetent cervix, persistent second- or third-trimester bleeding, placenta previa after 26 weeks, preeclampsia/gestational hypertension).
  - Gestational safety boundaries & Immediate termination red flags (ACOG 804):
    - Maternal thermal threshold: Avoid maternal core temperature exceeding 38.3 degrees Celsius (101 degrees Fahrenheit); avoid exercise in humid or high-heat environments to protect fetal neural development. Avoid supine exercise positions after the first trimester to prevent aortocaval compression by the gravid uterus.
    - **Immediate exercise termination red flags (Stop activity and seek medical evaluation):**
      - Vaginal bleeding or fluid leakage from the vagina.
      - Regular, painful uterine contractions.
      - Dyspnea prior to initiating exertion.
      - Dizziness, presyncope, or headache.
      - Chest pain or acute unexplained tachycardia.
      - Calf pain or asymmetric lower-extremity swelling (must rule out deep vein thrombosis, as thromboembolic risk is elevated fourfold to fivefold during pregnancy and the puerperium).
  - Lactational hypoestrogenism & Bone Stress Injury (BSI) surveillance:
    - Exclusive lactation stimulates parathyroid hormone-related protein (PTHrP), transiently increasing bone resorption to supply calcium for breast milk. Resuming running in lactating athletes requires strict screening for Low Energy Availability (LEA) and adequate calcium (1,000 to 1,300 mg daily) and vitamin D to prevent sacral and femoral neck stress fractures.
    - Lochia surveillance: Resurgence of bright red lochia or suddenly heavier vaginal bleeding following physical exertion serves as a clinical marker of excessive uterine vascular strain or subinvolution requiring training deload.
