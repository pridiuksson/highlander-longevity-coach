### Sarcopenia Prevention & Functional Independence (Elder 60+)

- **Vitality Ceiling (Salutogenesis, Neuromuscular Power & Functional Autonomy):**
  - High-Velocity Resistance Training (HVRT) & Type II motor unit defense:
    - Neuromuscular power decline outpaces the loss of isometric strength by twofold to threefold with aging due to preferential denervation and atrophy of Type II fast-twitch motor units (Reid et al., 2012).
    - Protocol: Explosive concentric actions (intent to move rapidly against moderate loads of 40% to 60% 1RM) combined with functional loaded carries (farmer's walks) stimulate motor unit recruitment velocity and protective trip-recovery reflexes.
  - Anabolic resistance nutrition & Bioenergetics (Bauer et al., PROT-AGE 2013):
    - Daily protein target: 1.2 to 1.5 grams of complete protein per kilogram body weight daily (up to 2.0 grams during acute or chronic inflammatory stress) to overcome age-related splanchnic extraction and blunted muscle protein synthesis.
    - Per-meal leucine threshold: Boluses of 2.5 to 3.0 grams of leucine (0.40 grams of complete protein per kilogram body weight per feeding) distributed across three to four meals.
    - Creatine monohydrate: 3 to 5 grams daily to support phosphocreatine resynthesis, strength retention, and neurocognitive protection.
  - Multimodal dynamic balance & Postural stability (Fielding et al., LIFE Study 2014):
    - Dynamic postural control: Multimodal balance challenges (tandem stance, single-leg balance, vestibular head turns) performed at least three days weekly to preserve agility and functional mobility.
- **Clinical Floor (Harm Reduction, Diagnostic Staging & Renal Safety):**
  - Sarcopenia screening & Diagnostic staging (EWGSOP2 Consensus; Cruz-Jentoft et al., 2019):
    - Case-finding screening: Initiate formal clinical workup if SARC-F questionnaire score is four or greater (evaluating strength, assistance walking, rising from a chair, climbing stairs, and falls).
    - Probable sarcopenia: Evaluated via isometric grip strength (diagnostic cut-off: under twenty-seven kilograms for men, under sixteen kilograms for women) or 5-Time Sit-to-Stand test (5XSTS time exceeding 15 seconds).
    - Confirmed sarcopenia: Documented low muscle strength plus low muscle quantity measured via dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (appendicular skeletal muscle mass [ASMM] index cut-off under 7.0 kilograms per square meter in men and under 5.5 kilograms per square meter in women).
    - Severe sarcopenia: Low muscle strength, low muscle quantity, and impaired physical performance (usual gait speed at or below 0.8 meters per second).
  - Renal safety baseline & Biomarker monitoring:
    - Mandatory baseline renal panel: Check estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) prior to escalating protein intake. Unmonitored high-protein diets are contraindicated in severe renal disease (eGFR under 30 mL/min/1.73m², CKD Stage 4–5 not on dialysis; PROT-AGE guidelines).
    - Serum Cystatin-C monitoring: Evaluate alongside standard kidney panels, as oral creatine supplementation can cause benign elevations in serum creatinine without reflecting true filtration impairment.
  - Medication audit (2023 AGS Beers Criteria):
    - Deprescribing audit: Routine screen for potentially inappropriate medications in older adults. Prioritize identifying sedatives/hypnotics (benzodiazepines, Z-drugs), sulfonylureas (elevated risk of severe hypoglycemic falls), high-anticholinergic burden drugs, and vasodilators causing orthostatic hypotension.
