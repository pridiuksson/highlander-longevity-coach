### Concurrent Athlete (Hybrid Training Architecture)

- **Vitality Ceiling (Salutogenesis, Dual-Pathway Adaptation & Connective Tissue Remodeling):**
  - Molecular signaling optimization (AMPK vs. mTORC1 kinetics; Baar, *Sports Med* 2014; Coffey & Hawley, *J Physiol* 2017):
    - Simultaneous stimulation of aerobic capacity and muscular hypertrophy requires deliberate temporal separation. Endurance exercise activates AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK), phosphorylating TSC2 and Raptor to blunt downstream mTORC1 signaling, while SIRT1 functions as an NAD⁺-dependent deacetylase to deacetylate PGC-1alpha, driving mitochondrial biogenesis.
    - *Endurance prioritized:* Perform high-intensity endurance training early in the day, followed by at least three to six hours of recovery and carbohydrate refueling to normalize AMPK activity prior to resistance training.
    - *Hypertrophy/Strength prioritized:* Execute resistance training first to maximize mechanical tension; separate the subsequent endurance session by at least six to eight hours or place on alternate days to protect the nascent mTORC1 protein synthesis cascade.
  - Volume landmarks & Leucine signaling (Swinton et al., *Sports Med* 2024; Bagheri et al., *Sports Med* 2022; Schoenfeld et al., 2017):
    - Weekly set-volume periodization programmed between Minimum Effective Volume (MEV) and Maximum Recoverable Volume (MRV) per muscle group, leveraging dose-response modeling across strength and conditioning domains.
    - Leucine threshold & protein timing: Muscle protein synthesis remains elevated 24 to 48 hours post-resistance loading; total daily protein intake (1.6 to 2.2 g/kg/day) dominates adaptation over a rigid acute window. Ingesting 0.3 to 0.4 grams of complete protein per kilogram body mass containing at least three grams of leucine post-training serves as an effective practical adherence anchor and stimulates downstream S6K1 and 4E-BP1 signaling.
  - Connective tissue remodeling & Tendon kinetics (Shaw, Baar et al., *Am J Clin Nutr* 2017):
    - Tendon and ligament collagen synthesis requires 48 to 72 hours of cellular matrix remodeling post-heavy eccentric or plyometric loading.
    - Nutritional pre-loading: Ingest fifteen grams of hydrolyzed collagen or gelatin with fifty milligrams of vitamin C thirty to sixty minutes prior to loading to enrich synovial fluid with proline and hydroxyproline during tissue perfusion.
- **Clinical Floor (Harm Reduction, Autonomic Overreaching & Injury Surveillance):**
  - Autonomic nervous system recovery gating (Plews et al., *Sports Med* 2013):
    - Telemetry: Monitored via the natural log of RMSSD (rolling seven-day mean and coefficient of variation) compared against an established thirty-day longitudinal baseline.
    - Bidirectional Smallest Worthwhile Change (SWC) gating:
      - *Sympathetic fatigue pattern:* A depression in rolling autonomic markers below 0.5 to 1.5 standard deviations indicates acute autonomic strain; substitute high-intensity intervals or heavy eccentric lifts with Zone 1 active recovery.
      - *Parasympathetic hyperactivity / saturation pattern:* An abnormal elevation in rolling markers above 1.5 standard deviations accompanied by persistent resting bradycardia and blunted heart rate response during exercise indicates autonomic exhaustion; warrants immediate volume reduction and active deload.
  - Training modality tracking:
    - Endurance modality: <RUNNING / CYCLING / ROWING / SWIMMING>, `<WEEKLY_DISTANCE_OR_HOURS>`.
    - Resistance modality: <POWERLIFTING / HYPERTROPHY / OLYMPIC LIFTING>, `<SESSIONS_PER_WEEK>`.
